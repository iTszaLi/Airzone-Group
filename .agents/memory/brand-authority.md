---
name: Airzone brand hierarchy
description: User-defined identity and approval boundaries for Airzone corporate and division artwork.
---

AIRZONE GROUP is the primary corporate identity. AIRZONE LOGISTICS and AIRZONE
TRAVELS & TOURISM are separate divisions; a division logo must not replace the
master logo in the global website header or favicon.

**Why:** The user explicitly supplied the three official logos and required their
exact designs, lettering, symbols, proportions and colours to be preserved. They
asked for clearer presentation, not new artwork.

**How to apply:** Use the supplied assets and restrained deterministic cleanup.
Do not redraw, retype, recolour or invent missing detail. Keep an appropriate
white backing when no official dark-background variant exists. Never call
enlarged low-resolution originals native 4K. Asset provenance and processing are
documented in `docs/branding-assets.md`.

For Logistics, use only the user-approved cropped artwork with the English name
and globe. Do not restore the Arabic registration-details panel from the earlier
letterhead image.

**Why:** The user supplied a replacement crop and said “use only this much logo
for logistics.”

**How to apply:** Keep this crop across Logistics pages, quotation forms and
confirmation states; leave the Group and Travel logos unchanged.

Browser favicons must use the Group logo in a circular disc with transparent
outer corners. This treatment is for the browser icon, not the website's
displayed logos. Keep Apple's touch icon separate from browser icon geometry.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected the square white browser-tab background
and CSS-only rounding. Browser favicon caches can also retain the previous icon.

**How to apply:** Generate genuinely transparent circular PNG/ICO and SVG
container assets, and give changed favicon content fresh, matching versioned
URLs so it does not depend on users manually clearing the previous icon cache.
