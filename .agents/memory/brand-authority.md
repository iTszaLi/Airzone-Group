---
name: Airzone brand hierarchy
description: User-defined identity and approval boundaries for Airzone corporate and division artwork.
---

AIRZONE GROUP is the primary corporate identity. AIRZONE LOGISTICS and AIRZONE
TRAVELS & TOURISM are separate divisions; a division logo must not replace the
master logo in the global website header or favicon.

**Why:** The user explicitly supplied the three official logos and required their
exact designs, lettering, symbols, proportions and colours to be preserved. They
asked for clearer presentation, not new artwork.

**How to apply:** Use the supplied assets and restrained deterministic cleanup.
Do not redraw, retype, recolour or invent missing detail. Keep an appropriate
white backing when no official dark-background variant exists. Never call
enlarged low-resolution originals native 4K. Asset provenance and processing are
documented in `docs/branding-assets.md`.
