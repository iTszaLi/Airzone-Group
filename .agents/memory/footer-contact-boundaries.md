name: Footer contact boundaries
description: User-defined division contact ownership and approval requirements, without storing contact details.
---

Business contact details must be visibly separated by division. The supplied
Travel office must never be presented as a Logistics office. Logistics location
and phone remain unconfirmed until the user supplies them; only the country may
be shown meanwhile.

**Why:** The footer brief explicitly distinguishes the two businesses and forbids
inventing Logistics addresses, map pins and phone numbers.

**How to apply:** Keep approved public values in the centralized business contact
configuration. Do not infer division ownership from generic Group settings.
Treat WhatsApp availability as a separate confirmation from phone ownership.
Display social accounts only when official URLs have been supplied.
