# Aviation story backgrounds

- clouds.webp: AI-generated image (Replit image generation, 2025). Prompt: soft realistic cumulus cloud bank, background removed. Decorative only.
- ground.webp: AI-generated image (Replit image generation, 2025). Prompt: aerial view of desert edge meeting a logistics port and highway grid, morning haze. Illustrative, does not depict a real Airzone site.

Both were resized to 1600px wide and converted to WebP. The aircraft is original procedural Three.js geometry. No third-party model is used.
