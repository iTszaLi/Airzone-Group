# AIRZONE GROUP — ADDITIONAL FEATURES & ENHANCEMENTS

Implement the following enhancements as part of the Airzone Group website architecture.

Prioritize functionality, reliability, performance and professional UX.

---

# HIGH PRIORITY FEATURES

## 1. ENGLISH + ARABIC MULTI-LANGUAGE SUPPORT

The website must support:

### English

Default language:

**EN**

### Arabic

Secondary language:

**AR**

Add a visible language switcher in the header:

**EN | AR**

When Arabic is selected:

* Switch the entire website to RTL
* Use appropriate Arabic typography
* Mirror navigation where appropriate
* Align text correctly
* Mirror icons/layouts where appropriate
* Keep forms fully RTL-compatible
* Keep images and visual composition natural
* Maintain the same premium design quality

The language selection should persist between pages.

Use a proper internationalization architecture rather than duplicating the entire application.

All important content should be translation-ready:

* Navigation
* Homepage
* Services
* Logistics pages
* Travel pages
* Forms
* Buttons
* Error messages
* Admin labels where applicable
* SEO metadata
* Footer
* Privacy/Terms pages

Architecture should allow additional languages later, such as:

* Hindi
* Kannada

Do NOT add additional languages to the visible interface until configured by the administrator.

# 2. WHATSAPP BUSINESS / LIVE CHAT

Prepare the website for official WhatsApp Business integration.

Create contextual WhatsApp CTAs.

For Logistics:

For Travel:

**Plan Your Trip on WhatsApp**

**Logistics**

# 28. GOOGLE REVIEW CONVERSION STRATEGY

Create a smart but ethical review flow.

After a completed service:

1. Customer receives thank-you message.
2. Customer sees:

**We'd love to hear about your experience.**

3. Customer clicks:

**Leave a Google Review**

4. Customer is redirected directly to Airzone's official Google review page.

Do NOT:

* ask customers to give 5 stars before opening Google
* hide the review option from unhappy customers
* create fake reviews
* incentivize positive reviews unless compliant with Google's policies
* copy Google reviews into the website automatically without permission

The objective is genuine customer feedback.
