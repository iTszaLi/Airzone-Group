# 7. PREMIUM SCROLL EXPERIENCE — GSAP + SCROLLTRIGGER

Use GSAP and ScrollTrigger where they materially improve the experience.

The animation system must be polished, lightweight and restrained.

Do not animate everything.

## Hero animation

- Headline reveals smoothly.
- Supporting text follows with a subtle stagger.
- CTA buttons appear naturally.
- Background imagery may use a restrained, slow reveal or subtle parallax.

## Business division animation

- Images reveal through elegant masks or clip-path transitions.
- Text enters with a subtle vertical movement.
- Stagger the two divisions without making users wait to access content.

## Service section animation

- Headlines reveal when entering the viewport.
- Images use restrained reveal transitions.
- Service lists appear with subtle staggered motion.

## Horizontal storytelling

Where useful, create one controlled horizontal storytelling section on desktop.

Do not force horizontal scrolling across the whole website.

On mobile, convert horizontal interactions to natural vertical scrolling.

## CEO section animation

- Photograph enters with a subtle reveal.
- The CEO message fades or slides into place.
- Signature or name appears last, if an authentic signature asset is supplied.

## Footer transition

Create a graceful transition into the final CTA and footer.

### Technical requirements

- Use GSAP with ScrollTrigger.
- Register plugins correctly.
- Clean up animations when React components unmount.
- Avoid duplicate ScrollTriggers during navigation.
- Use transform/opacity-based animations where practical.
- Avoid expensive continuous layout recalculations.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Disable or simplify parallax on small screens and lower-powered devices.
- Never hide critical content if an animation fails.
- Ensure content is readable before animations run.
- Avoid scroll hijacking.
- Avoid pinned sections that trap users or interfere with browser scrolling.

The website must remain fast and accessible even when animations are disabled.
