# AIRZONE GROUP — COMPLETE SEO, LOCAL SEO, AEO, GEO & CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION IMPLEMENTATION

## PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Upgrade the existing Airzone Group website at **airzonegroup.com** into a technically sound, search-optimized, high-converting corporate website for its two specialist businesses:

1. **Airzone Logistics** — customs clearance, ATA Carnet assistance, exhibition and event logistics, import/export support, freight forwarding and cargo coordination in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia and relevant GCC markets.
2. **Airzone Travel & Tourism** — domestic and international travel assistance, holiday planning and corporate travel services.

The immediate commercial priority is the Logistics division.

The website must be discoverable by traditional search engines and easier for AI-powered search systems to understand, retrieve and accurately reference.

Target platforms include Google Search, Bing, ChatGPT search, Google Gemini and Perplexity, without promising guaranteed rankings, citations or AI mentions.

**Implement these improvements directly in the existing codebase. Do not just produce an SEO audit or a list of recommendations.**

Preserve the existing design, GSAP animations, bilingual English/Arabic experience, business logos, forms, database, authentication, admin panel and existing functionality.
