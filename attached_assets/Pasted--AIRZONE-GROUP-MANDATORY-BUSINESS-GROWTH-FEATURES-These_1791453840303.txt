# AIRZONE GROUP — MANDATORY BUSINESS GROWTH FEATURES

These features are not optional design ideas. Build the architecture and UI so they are functional and manageable through the admin dashboard.

---

# 1. WHATSAPP BUSINESS CONVERSION SYSTEM

Implement separate WhatsApp conversion flows for the two Airzone divisions.

## LOGISTICS

CTA:

**Talk to Logistics**

or

**Chat with a Logistics Expert**

Use a configurable WhatsApp number from Admin Settings.

Pre-filled message example:

> Hello Airzone Logistics, I would like assistance with a logistics/customs clearance requirement.

For specific services, dynamically generate a more relevant message.

Example:

> Hello Airzone Logistics, I need customs clearance assistance for a shipment from [Origin] to [Destination].

---

## TRAVEL & TOURISM

CTA:

**Plan My Trip on WhatsApp**

Pre-filled message:

> Hello Airzone Travels & Tourism, I would like assistance planning my trip.

If the customer is viewing a package:

> Hello Airzone Travel Team, I am interested in the [Package Name] package.

Do not hardcode phone numbers.

Store WhatsApp numbers in:

**Admin → Website Settings → WhatsApp**

Allow separate numbers for:

* Logistics
* Travel

Track WhatsApp clicks through analytics.
