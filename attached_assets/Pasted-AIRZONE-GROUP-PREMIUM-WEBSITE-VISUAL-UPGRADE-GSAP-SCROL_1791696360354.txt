- Airzone Logistics — customs clearance, freight and cargo coordination.
- Airzone Travel & Tourism — personal, corporate and leisure travel assistance.
The website should communicate trust, precision, movement, international connectivity and premium service.
Visual inspiration: the polish and restraint of Apple product pages, the strong imagery of premium travel brands, and the confident corporate presentation of international logistics companies.
Use these references for design direction only. Do not copy their layouts, assets or branding.
Visual style
- Deep navy as the primary corporate colour.
- Warm ivory and clean white for breathing room.
- Muted gold accents used sparingly.
- High-quality, cinematic photography.
- Strong typography with editorial-style headlines.
- Asymmetric layouts and generous whitespace.
- Beautiful image cropping and carefully controlled contrast.
- Refined transitions and subtle interactive details.
Avoid a generic template appearance, excessive cards, colourful gradients, oversized rounded boxes, unnecessary icons and repetitive sections.
The final result should feel custom-designed for Airzone Group.

4. GSAP SCROLLTRIGGER — CINEMATIC SCROLL STORYTELLING
Use GSAP and ScrollTrigger where they materially improve the experience.
First inspect the existing package configuration. If GSAP is not installed, add it using the package manager appropriate to the existing project.
Create a reusable animation system instead of scattering unrelated animation code across components.
A. Section entrance animations
Animate important sections as they enter the viewport:
- Eyebrow labels fade in.
- Headlines reveal with a restrained vertical movement.
- Body copy fades in smoothly.
- Images reveal through clean clipping or masking.
- CTAs appear after the main content.
Keep the movement subtle, typically around 16–32 pixels, with approximately 0.5–0.9-second transitions.
Avoid animating every individual word or element.
B. Business showcase
For the Logistics and Travel showcase, use an elegant image reveal and a subtle background-image scale transition as the section enters the viewport.
If a pinned sequence is used on desktop, keep it short, intuitive and easy to navigate. Disable or simplify pinning on mobile.
C. Numbered storytelling sections
The current numbered sections "01 — 03" can become a refined editorial storytelling experience.
Use them to introduce:
- A group built around two specialist businesses.
- Practical coordination and dependable service.
- A clear next step for customers.
Use a subtle progress indicator or understated line animation if it enhances navigation.
Do not let decorative numbers take up excessive space.
D. Scroll progress
Optionally add a very thin, restrained progress indicator near the top of the page.
It must not obstruct the navigation or distract visitors.
E. Navigation
Make the header feel premium and stable:
- Transparent or visually integrated over the hero where appropriate.
- Transition into a solid background when scrolling.
- Subtle shadow or border after scrolling, if necessary.
- Smooth mobile menu transitions.
- Clear active navigation states.

10. PERFORMANCE, ACCESSIBILITY AND MOTION SAFETY
Premium motion must not compromise performance.
Requirements:
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Disable or simplify parallax and scroll pinning when reduced motion is requested.
- Avoid layout shifts during animation.
- Avoid unnecessary JavaScript and oversized bundles.
- Clean up GSAP timelines and ScrollTrigger instances when components unmount.
- Prevent duplicate triggers during route changes or React Strict Mode.
- Ensure smooth scrolling works with keyboard and assistive technology.
- Maintain sufficient colour contrast.
- Keep forms accessible.
- Test on mid-range mobile devices.
- Ensure the website remains usable if animations fail to load.
Do not use smooth-scroll libraries that interfere with native browser scrolling unless they provide a clear benefit and are integrated correctly.
