## Also needs the owner

- **Knowledge Graph links (`sameAs`):** add the real LinkedIn, Instagram and other profile URLs to `socialLinks` in `config/business-contacts.ts`. They then appear in the footer and in the Organization structured data automatically.
- **Search Console:** after the site is live, check the generative-AI search setting and submit the sitemap.

## Not adopted (on purpose)

- **`/.well-known/ai.txt`, `/ai/summary.json`, `/ai/faq.json`:** these are proposals from the audit tool, not conventions the major AI engines have adopted.
- **RSS feed:** there are no articles or news.
- **"Hidden text" warning:** it refers to the closed navigation menus (normal UI), not hidden content.
